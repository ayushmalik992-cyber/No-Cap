Fair questions

No cap, here’s how it works.

Is NoCap accusing apps of fraud?

No. It shows you exactly what changed between two checkouts and which CCPA dark-pattern guideline it resembles. Whether to file is your call.

What data leaves my phone?

Comparisons run on-device. Only anonymised aggregates (zone, fee type, amount) are shared to power the heatmap. No individual checkout is ever shown.

Where does the “clean” baseline come from?

From sessions captured in the same PIN code at the same minute, with no purchase history or device signals attached.

What counts as shrinkflation?

Any pack whose net weight or volume dropped while the MRP stayed flat. We rank items by how much less you get, as a true cost per 100g or 100ml.
